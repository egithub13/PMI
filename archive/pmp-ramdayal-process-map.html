RAMDAYAL'S KEY STUDY STRATEGIES FOR THE 49 PROCESSES
1. Don't Memorize ITTOs Blindly: Understand WHY an output from one process becomes an input to another. The logic tells the story of a project lifecycle.
2. The PM Plan is King: "Develop PM Plan" collects subsidiary plans from every KA. It's the #1 hub — everything flows through it.
3. Master the Change Control Loop: Work → Change Requests → Integrated Change Control → Approved Changes → back to Direct & Manage. The exam LOVES this cycle.
4. Predictive vs Agile Mindset: In predictive, follow the plan and use formal change control. In agile, embrace change, empower the team, iterate. In hybrid, blend both — the exam tests whether you know WHEN to apply each.
5. Servant Leadership Always: The PM removes obstacles, supports the team, facilitates (doesn't dictate), and communicates proactively. This mindset answers 80% of situational questions.
6. Three Domains Matter: People (~42%), Process (~50%), Business Environment (~8%). Most processes map across domains — know which hat you're wearing.
